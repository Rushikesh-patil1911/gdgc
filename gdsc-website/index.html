We bring students together to learn, share, and build with Google technologies.

Google Developer Student Clubs are university-based communities for students interested in technology. At D.Y. Patil College of Engineering, we create a welcoming space to explore ideas, develop real-world skills, and turn curiosity into projects.

Meet our community →

GDSC is a grassroots community where students learn development skills and build solutions together.

Every student is welcome—from curious beginners to experienced developers ready for a new challenge.

Meet collaborators, learn by doing, and get the confidence to bring your next idea to life.

Join sessions, contribute to projects, and find your people in a supportive developer community.
